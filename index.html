About JNPHS

Jawahar Nagar Pinion High School (JNPHS) was established in 1997 with a commitment to provide quality education to middle-class families in the vicinity of Jawahar Nagar, Secunderabad. Over 21+ successful SSC batches, we have maintained a 100% pass record, building a legacy of trust, discipline, and academic excellence.

Our Vision

To impart quality education through dedicated teachers and management, with a special focus on middle-class aspirants at reasonable cost — empowering every student to grow into a responsible, educated citizen.

Our Mission

To nurture students with strong academic foundations, moral values, and leadership qualities — guided by our motto: "Come to Learn, Go to Serve."
