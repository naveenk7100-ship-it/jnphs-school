About JNPHS

Motto: "Come to Learn, Go to Serve" • Established 1997

Foundational Vision

Our Official Vision

"The JNPHS is established in the year 1997 with a vision to impart quality education by dedicated Teachers and Management with a special focus on middle class aspirants living in the vicinity of Jawahar Nagar at reasonable cost."

Our commitment remains steadfast: ensuring every student receives rigorous academic training, character building, and individual attention under our motto "Come to Learn, Go to Serve".

Traditional Motto & Blessing

అఖండ విద్యాప్రాప్తిరస్తు (Akhanda Vidya Praptirastu)
